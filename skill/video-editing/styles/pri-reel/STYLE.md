# Style: pri-reel (@buildwithpri, "talking head + screen cut-ins")

Use this style by default for Pri's videos. Everything below is measured from her own reference edit and her approved v2. It is encoded in `scripts/reel_engine.py`, so most of it needs no manual work: you only supply an edit spec (see `example/spec.json`).

## Look (1080x1920, 30 fps)
| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Background | Pure black |
| Title | 2 lines, top, butter yellow `#FFF1A8`, Inter Black 68 px, centered at y≈290. Pops in over 0.4 s. Stays for the whole video. Default text: "Cool ChatGPT / tricks & shortcuts" (change per video) |
| Underline | Thin pastel blue `#BFD9F2` line under the title, draws outward from the center |
| Command line | Monospace, butter yellow, typed character by character with a pastel-blue caret, e.g. `/billboard`. Erases before the next command is typed. It names whatever is being demoed at that moment |
| Speaker window | Full width, 1080x886 at y=565. Speaker centered on the face, never the top half of the frame |
| Cut-ins | Screen recording replaces the speaker window over a blurred, darkened copy of the speaker. Rounded corners + soft shadow. First show the chat/prompt (phone cropped to the content, no status bar), then **reveal the result large** (wide results fill the width, tall results fill the height) |
| Captions | y≈1630, white, Inter Bold 70, 2-3 words at a time, lowercase, spoken word in butter yellow. Words are corrected (ChatGPT, DMs, /command) |
| Outro | ~2.4 s card in the same window: pastel-blue pill with the CTA ("Comment 200"), two white lines, handle in pastel blue. Command line types the keyword |
| Safe zone | Nothing important below y=1700 or above y=200 (Instagram UI) |

## Motion
- Speaker window drifts in/out 0-7% per cut, alternating direction so every cut feels different.
- Zoom punch (+7.5%, 0.22 s decay) on the key word of each beat ("billboard", "foodshot", the CTA number).
- Cut-ins scale in from 88% with a soft overshoot; result reveals pop from 94%; results push in slowly.
- Hard cuts only. No fancy transitions.

## Pacing
- **Speed 1.2-1.3x** (default 1.25). Cut every stumble, restart, repeated sentence, "um" and filler. Keep the real CTA and the sign-off ("okay, bye").
- **Under 35 s total**, including outro. Say the number: report the final length.
- The first line must be the hook. If the speaker restarts a sentence, keep the cleaner take.
- Cut-ins start when the speaker **names** the thing, and the result appears when they say what it does.

## Sound
- Voice first: high-pass 85 Hz, de-click, mild noise reduction, mud cut at 220 Hz, presence +1.5 dB at 3.2 kHz, **split-band compression above 4.5 kHz to tame harsh t/s/p bursts** (found in her recordings: "prompt", "shortcuts"), 8 ms fades at every cut, voice to -16 LUFS.
- Music: uplifting, major key, ~118 BPM, ducked under the voice with sidechain compression. Generated by default (no copyright); a licensed MP3 can be supplied in the spec.
- SFX (soft, never crisp): typing ticks, whoosh on cut-in, pop on result reveal, bell on outro.
- Final mix -14 LUFS, true peak below -1.5 dB.

## Palette
Butter yellow `#FFF1A8`, pastel blue `#BFD9F2`, navy `#1F3A5F` (text on pastel blue). These are estimates: confirm with Pri's real brand hex codes and update `colors` in the spec.

## Learned the hard way (check every time)
1. **Check orientation first.** Phone files can be stored sideways with no rotation flag. Extract one still (`ffmpeg -ss 3 -i in.mp4 -frames:v 1 f.png`), look at it, and set `rotate` to `cw`/`ccw`/`none`.
2. **Scan the screen recording for private/ugly moments** before choosing cut-in times: notification banners, the pulled-down notification shade, keyboards, other apps. Pick clean seconds.
3. **Listen for harsh sounds** by measuring: windows that are >70% energy above 4 kHz while loud. After the fix they must sit well below the speech body (about -37 dB vs -15 dB).
4. **Never claim it sounds good.** Report measured loudness/peak and ask the user to listen.
5. **Wide landscape source in a reel:** keep the speaker window wide (this style) instead of center-cropping to 9:16. Face stays big, nothing is zoomed 2.7x.
6. **Don't edit before a storyboard is approved.** See SKILL.md.
